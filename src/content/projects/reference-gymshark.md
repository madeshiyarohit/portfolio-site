## The challenge for a store like this

Fitness apparel moves fast: new drops, limited colours and trends driven by athletes and social media. The store needs to launch collections quickly, keep returning visitors excited and still make it easy to find the right fit.

## Homepage walkthrough

1. **Named product lines as heroes.** *SoftSense™* and *Vengeance* get top billing, with feature highlights like the *dipped waistband*.
2. **Athlete collaboration.** A dedicated collaboration block connects products to people the audience follows.
3. **Popular Right Now.** Trending products for shoppers who want to see what others are buying.
4. **Lifestyle collections.** Loungewear and outfit sets (for example colour-matched gym sets) widen the reasons to buy.

## Catalog and navigation

- **Collections are brands of their own.** Shoppers browse by named lines (and their features), not just "leggings" or "tops".
- **A content management system (Contentful)** suggests homepage and campaign content is managed separately from products, so marketing can launch campaigns quickly.

## Products and merchandising

- Features (waistband styles, fabrics) are marketed like products in their own right.
- **Sets and colour stories** encourage buying multiple pieces together.
- Trend sections update often to keep the homepage fresh.

## Trust and conversion

- **A clear free-delivery goal ($75)** encourages adding one more item.
- **Self-service returns and exchanges (Loop)** make it easier to order a size you're unsure about.

## Ideas I'd test

These are my own suggestions, not facts about the store:

- A cart progress bar showing how close the shopper is to free delivery.
- Fit notes pulled from reviews, shown next to the size selector.
- "Complete the set" suggestions on product pages.

## What this teaches for your own case studies

- For trend-driven brands, explain **how launches and campaigns are run**.
- Show how **merchandising by collection** guides shoppers.
- Mention the **tools that let the team move fast** (content system, returns app).
