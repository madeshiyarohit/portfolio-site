## The challenge for a store like this

Rugs are big, visual and expensive-feeling purchases, and many people avoid nice rugs because of spills, pets and kids. The store has to make the washable benefit instantly believable, help shoppers find the right style **and size** in a huge range, and remove worries about shipping something bulky.

## Homepage walkthrough

1. **The problem, named.** The store description speaks to homes with kids and pets: the exact people who worry about rugs.
2. **The benefit as peace of mind.** "Machine washable and stain resistant" is framed as a lifestyle benefit, not a technical spec.
3. **A clear promotion.** "Up to 40% off" is big, simple and easy to understand.
4. **Free shipping.** Especially important for a large, heavy item.

## Catalog and navigation

- **Dedicated search and filtering tools** (Searchspring, Constructor) suggest a large catalog where filtering by size, color, style and room matters a lot.
- **A content management system (Contentful)** lets the team build inspiration and campaign pages separately from products.

## Products and merchandising

- Rugs come in many sizes and shapes, so **size selection** is a key part of the product page.
- Style-led browsing (patterns, colors, rooms) helps people picture the rug at home.

## Trust and conversion

- The **washable benefit** is repeated consistently, so it becomes the brand's identity.
- **Free shipping** removes a common reason people abandon carts for bulky items.
- **Reviews** are part of the store experience, important for a product people can't touch first.

## Ideas I'd test

These are my own suggestions, not facts about the store:

- A room-size helper that recommends a rug size from room measurements.
- Short before/after clips of a rug being cleaned.
- "See it in your room" photos from customers on product pages.

## What this teaches for your own case studies

- Start with the **customer's real-life problem**, then show how every part of the store answers it.
- Explain how you made a **big catalog easy to filter**.
- For bulky or high-ticket products, show how you **removed shipping and fit worries**.
